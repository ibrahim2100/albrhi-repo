Albrhi for TikTok

No ads, downloads, privacy, and TikTok's own checks answered the way an unmodified phone would. . ▍NO ADS A feed item TikTok's own server marked as an ad never becomes an object at all — refused at the model itself, the moment it is built, rather than a view hidden after the fact. A separate splash-ad surface is suppressed too. . ▍DOWNLOAD One button, right beside like, comment and share, on the video actually on screen. Saved straight into Photos — no list to open, no second place to look. . ▍PRIVACY Three separate switches, not one — a story's seen mark, a message's read receipt and a profile view report to three different places, so turning one off never touches the other two. Never touches what this device's own screen shows. . ▍HIDES THE JAILBREAK TikTok asks many places whether the device is modified: a direct jailbreak check, its signing information, whether the app looks like a genuine App Store build, whether an embedded provisioning profile exists, and more. Each is answered the way it would be on an unmodified phone. Nothing here touches a purchase, a receipt or a subscription — deliberately, even where a reference tweak names a jailbreak check living inside that surface. . ▍SEE WHAT IT CAUGHT Hold two fingers anywhere in TikTok for a real settings screen: a switch and an explanation for every feature, and live numbers behind each hook. Arabic and English. . TESTED ON TikTok 46.4.0. Nothing is pinned to a version number: every class is looked up at runtime and skipped if it is not there. . Architecture was read from BandarHL's and al3raQe's published BHTikTok source (github.com/BandarHL/BHTikTok, github.com/al3raQe/BHTikTok) and from two further compiled references, NA9 For TikTok and VibeTok — none carry a LICENSE file, so all are read for where TikTok is hookable only, the same cautious way this project treats every unlicensed reference. No code is taken from any of them; every class named here was independently confirmed present in TikTok's own binary before being hooked. . Developed by Ibrahim Ismail AL-Rahn.
