Albrhi for WhatsApp

Privacy for WhatsApp, in your hands. Albrhi only ever withholds: it holds back what would leave your phone, or refuses a delete order that arrives. It never sends anything untrue. Every switch is off until you turn it on, and says in one line what it costs.

البرهي للواتساب: خصوصيتك بيدك. الأداة تمنع فقط: تمسك اللي كان بيطلع من جوالك، أو ترفض أمر حذف وصلها. ما ترسل شي غير صحيح أبداً. كل مفتاح مطفي لين تشغّله، ويقول لك سطر واحد وش ثمنه.

Tested on WhatsApp 26.44.72, on a jailbroken iPhone (roothide) and in the iOS Simulator. Verified means watched working end to end; beta means hooked on the right path and counted, but not yet watched from the other side. The settings page shows a BETA tag until a feature has been.

No blue ticks beta

They do not see blue ticks when you read. You still see theirs. Separate from WhatsApp's own switch.

No typing or recording beta

The other side is not told that you are typing or recording.

No “played” on voice notes beta

You listen and the sender's microphone does not turn blue.

Keep deleted messages verified

A message deleted for everyone stays on this phone, with a small red bin beside it. Tap the bin for the whole story of the deletion.

Edited messages beta

A small pencil beside an edited message opens every version: before, after, and when.

View once: view again beta

View-once photos and videos open like ordinary media. The sender is not told until you press the eye.

Keep disappearing messages unproven

Timed messages stay, with a small hourglass. Installed and the app runs normally; not yet seen on a message that really expired.

Statuses beta · save verified

No “seen” on statuses, deleted statuses stay, and the viewer gets an eye (mark seen when you choose) and an arrow (save to Photos).

Per person and per group beta

Each privacy switch can apply to everyone, only to chosen chats, or to everyone except them. A card at the top of a contact's info page does it for that person.

Forwarding beta

Hide the “Forwarded” label, and an ∞ button that lifts the five-chat forward limit. Heavy forwarding can get an account restricted: the switch says so.

Profiles and search works

Ghost, Quiet and Keeper set the switches in one go. Search finds any switch by its words, in Arabic or English.
