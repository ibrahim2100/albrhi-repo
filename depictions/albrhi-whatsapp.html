Albrhi for WhatsApp

Privacy and comfort for WhatsApp, in your hands. The privacy switches only withhold: they hold back what would leave your phone, or refuse a delete order that arrives, and never send anything untrue. The other features (status row, swipe buttons, themes, app lock, hidden chats, seconds in times, scheduled messages…) are what you choose to turn on, and the few that send something or change what the other side sees say so on their page. Every switch is off until you turn it on, and says in one line what it costs.

البرهي للواتساب: خصوصيتك وراحتك بيدك. مفاتيح الخصوصية تمنع فقط: تمسك اللي كان بيطلع من جوالك، أو ترفض أمر حذف وصلها، وما ترسل شي غير صحيح أبداً. وباقي المزايا (صف الحالات، أزرار السحب، الثيمات، قفل التطبيق، المحادثات المخفية، الثواني في الأوقات، الرسائل المجدولة…) تشغّلها بنفسك، واللي يرسل شي أو يغيّر اللي يشوفه الطرف الثاني يقول لك في صفحته. كل مفتاح مطفي لين تشغّله، ويقول لك سطر واحد وش ثمنه.

Tested on WhatsApp 26.44.72, on a jailbroken iPhone (roothide) and in the iOS Simulator. Verified means watched working end to end; beta means hooked on the right path and counted, but not yet watched from the other side. The settings page shows a BETA tag until a feature has been.

No blue ticks beta

They do not see blue ticks when you read. You still see theirs. Separate from WhatsApp's own switch.

No typing or recording beta

The other side is not told that you are typing or recording.

No “played” on voice notes beta

You listen and the sender's microphone does not turn blue.

Keep deleted messages verified

A message deleted for everyone stays on this phone, with a small red bin beside it. Tap the bin for the whole story of the deletion.

Edited messages beta

A small pencil beside an edited message opens every version: before, after, and when.

View once: view again beta

View-once photos and videos open like ordinary media. The sender is not told until you press the eye.

Keep disappearing messages unproven

Timed messages stay, with a small hourglass. Installed and the app runs normally; not yet seen on a message that really expired.

Statuses beta · save verified

No “seen” on statuses, deleted statuses stay, and the viewer gets an eye (mark seen when you choose) and an arrow (save to Photos).

Per person and per group beta

Each privacy switch can apply to everyone, only to chosen chats, or to everyone except them. A card at the top of a contact's info page does it for that person.

Forwarding beta

Hide the “Forwarded” label, and an ∞ button that lifts the five-chat forward limit. Heavy forwarding can get an account restricted: the switch says so.

Deleted statuses are marked verified

A status that was deleted and is still here shows a red bin and when it was deleted, beside its time in the viewer.

Status row in Chats verified

Everybody's statuses in a row right under the search bar: a green ring until you watch, yours first, new on the left and watched ones at the end. A tap opens WhatsApp's own viewer.

Chat swipe buttons verified

Choose the buttons under a swipe on a chat (delete, clear, mute, read, lock, favourite, block, hide, archive, more) and their order; the last one runs on a full swipe. WhatsApp asks before delete, clear and block.

Themes beta

Eleven colour themes (Ocean, Royal, Rose, Sunset, Gold, Graphite, Midnight…) and pure black in dark mode, by recolouring WhatsApp's green. Colours, not a re-skin: wallpapers and pictures that are green stay.

Seconds in times verified

Message times, the chat list, the status viewer and more show seconds (9:43:27 AM).

App lock and hidden chats verified

Face ID or your passcode to open WhatsApp; chosen chats leave the list and open after Face ID.

Ask first verified

“Call X?”, “Send this sticker?”, “Send this voice message?” before it happens.

Hide tabs, clean look verified

Take Updates, Calls or Communities out of the bottom bar; no floating Meta AI button; the search bar says “Search”.

Scheduled messages and auto-reply beta · scheduled verified

Write now and send at a time you pick (WhatsApp must be running); an auto-reply for chats you choose. They send real messages from your account: try them on a chat you control.

Fake location, HD photos, stay alive, delivery ticks, backup beta

Pick the place “Send my location” reads (the other side sees it, so use it knowing that); send photos at full quality; keep WhatsApp running in the background (it costs battery); hold back the delivery tick; back up and restore your settings.

Profiles and search works

Ghost, Quiet and Keeper set the switches in one go. Search finds any switch by its words, in Arabic or English.
